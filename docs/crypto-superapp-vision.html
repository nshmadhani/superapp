Vision & Opportunity

Ervo

One home for crypto — where trust, understanding, and onboarding finally live in the same place.

An agent-native consolidation layer that replaces the dozen apps, chains, and venues crypto currently forces on every user.

01 — The Problem

Crypto isn't hard. It's scattered.

Crypto is fragmented — and not just its liquidity. It's fragmented in attention.

Different apps to invest through. Different venues to trade on. Different chains to hold assets on. Different UIs to learn. Different accounts to trust. Power users push through it anyway — they've built up the muscle memory to tolerate the fragmentation. Everyone else looks at that sprawl and simply doesn't onboard.

It's not that crypto is too hard to understand. It's that there's no single place to stand while you try to understand it.

100+ actively used chains fragmenting where assets and liquidity live
1000s of protocols, venues, and apps a user is expected to individually vet and trust
1 consolidated, trusted layer that exists today to tie it all together — none

Illustrative figures on ecosystem sprawl — directional, not a cited market study.

02 — The Vision

The consolidation layer crypto doesn't have yet.

Ervo is one home where trust, understanding, and onboarding all live in the same place.

You say what you want — research something, check your portfolio, move funds, set up something recurring — and an agent figures out where things actually live and does it, across wallets, chains, and venues, through integrations that have already been vetted.

No app-hopping. No separately trusting a dozen protocols. No relearning a new UI for every new thing you want to do.

What Ervo absorbs on the user's behalf: trust. If a provider has already integrated an app, protocol, or venue, that's a signal it's been vetted. Not everyone gets access to everything from day one — what's exposed is curated. The platform carries that trust decision so the user doesn't have to.

03 — For Newcomers

You don't need to learn crypto to use Ervo.

If crypto has always felt like a wall of jargon, wallets, and warnings, that's the exact problem Ervo exists to remove — not by dumbing it down, but by standing between you and the complexity.

Just ask. Tell Ervo what you're trying to do in plain language — it figures out the rest and explains what it's doing along the way. Nothing moves without you saying yes, so there's no wrong click that costs you money. You start small, go at your own pace, and never have to touch an app or protocol Ervo hasn't already vetted.

04 — Why Now & The Opportunity

The moment — and the prize.

Wallet infrastructure (programmable wallets, account abstraction, relayers and paymasters) and agent capability (open-ended research, routing intent, resolving ambiguity) both matured at the same time — and confirm-gated execution makes it safe to combine them. No one has built the consolidated, trusted layer on top of that yet.

Whoever becomes that trusted front door doesn't just capture usage — they capture the relationship. New chains and protocols gain an incentive to be one of the integrations Ervo vets, the same way distribution matters on any platform users already trust.

05 — Who It's For

Everybody trying to actually use crypto — not just watch it.

  • —Active retail traders and power users who are tired of juggling apps and want one place to move faster.
  • —People just getting into crypto who need one place to understand how to onboard at all — the audience today's fragmentation locks out.
  • —Not professional or institutional traders who live in charts and terminals — that's a different job, and not the one Ervo is built for.

Beyond that, it's intentionally broad: anyone who wants to hold, move, or grow crypto without becoming an expert in ten different apps first.

06 — What It Does

Give it the tools. Let it handle the ask.

Ervo is given enough tools and access to do whatever a user asks of it — research, portfolio visibility, moving money, trading, automations — and to do it seamlessly, without the user needing to know which underlying app or venue is doing the work.

The point isn't a fixed feature list; it's breadth. Research a position. Swap an asset. Catch an opportunity across venues. Park funds somewhere safe. Set up something to run on its own later. If it's a legitimate crypto task, it should be something you can just ask Ervo to handle.

07 — Why This Wins

The moat is trust, not features.

1 Agent-led, not dashboard-led

The interface is intent, not navigation — no feature parity race with every underlying app.

2 Curated by design

Every integration is vetted before it's exposed — the platform absorbs a decision users don't want to make themselves.

3 Explicit trust boundary

Every wallet, venue, and fee is named before anything signs — confirm-gated execution, always.

4 One relationship, many rails

Wallets, chains, and venues are treated as plumbing behind one consistent home — new rails plug in without changing the experience.

08 — What Ervo Is Not

Not another tab. The home itself.

Ervo is not another place to trade — it's not one more app competing for a tab in your browser.

Ervo is meant to be the home for interacting with crypto: the one place you go, regardless of what you actually need to do. Done right, it shouldn't feel like "using a crypto app" at all — it should just feel like getting the thing you asked for done.

New Here — Start

What Ervo actually is, in plain terms.

Ervo is an app that lets you manage and use crypto by just asking for what you want, in plain English.

Think of it like texting a very capable, careful assistant who happens to know crypto inside and out. You don't need to know what a "chain" or a "wallet" is before you start — you just say what you're trying to do, and Ervo figures out the rest.

What's Actually Happening

Here's what happens when you ask it something.

1

You ask, in your own words

No special commands or vocabulary needed — "what do I own?" or "help me move my money somewhere safer" both work.

2

Ervo looks into it

It checks what you actually have, researches whatever it needs to, and works out how to do what you asked.

3

It shows you before doing anything

Plain-language summary of exactly what's about to happen — no hidden steps, no surprise fees.

4

You say yes or no

Nothing with your money happens until you approve it. You can always say no, or ask a question instead.

A Few Words You Might See

No need to memorize this — it's just here if you're curious.

Wallet

Your own account that holds crypto — like a bank account, except you're the only one who can approve what leaves it.

Chain / network

Different underlying systems crypto can live on — a bit like different banks that don't automatically talk to each other. Ervo handles moving between them.

Swap

Trading one kind of crypto for another, similar to exchanging currency at the airport.

Confirm

The one moment nothing happens without: a plain-language "here's what I'm about to do — okay?" that you have to approve.
